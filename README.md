## The word

> plansigh — the quiet relief when someone else cancels plans you secretly hoped would fall through.

98 characters, one line. *plansigh* (plan + sigh) names something English has no word for: the quiet, almost guilty relief when somebody else cancels plans you were dreading but would never cancel yourself. The word is sayable at once — two familiar syllables, spelled exactly as it sounds — and it performs its own meaning: the sigh is the feeling. The definition lands in one reading: "someone else cancels" does the work (you did not cancel, they did), "secretly hoped would fall through" names the part you would never admit out loud.
